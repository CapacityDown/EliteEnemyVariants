## v4.0.3
- Added the Elites menu with enemy enhancement descriptions, host-controlled selection switches, display refresh, and bug-report tools.
- Elites appears below Roles and Events when RoleShuffle or Stage Flux is installed.
- Added GitHub Issues guidance for questions, bug reports, and feedback.
- Increased the value of orbs dropped by defeated Enhanced enemies to 1.5 times their usual value.
- Animal prioritizes nearby clusters of valuables and loaded carts during havoc.
- Hidden can evade a visible rescuer once per capture without extending its carry time.
- Mentalist can reposition before charging to catch players and valuables together.
- Loom favors teleport destinations ahead of its new target while keeping distance from living players.
- Rugrat can move around cover with a held item to find a throwing angle.
- Headgrab can try once to recover a head dropped when stunned, giving up when a player rescues it.
- Tick can change its escape route once when a pursuer blocks its path after it fills up.
- Heart Hugger favors exits along the last-seen player's direction when reappearing after burrowing.
- Cleanup Crew can circle around the player opposite its thrown head while keeping clear of the bomb.
- Reaper checks one nearby corner after searching a player's last-seen position before ending its attack.

## v4.0.2
- Enemies spawned by Stage Flux can now become Enhanced.
- RoleShuffle Ninja, Vampire, and Hunter now work as intended with Enhanced enemies.

## v4.0.1
- Limited per-enemy chance settings to `-1` or whole percentages from 0 to 100.
- Added Threat Budget with a default limit of 4 while retaining the maximum Enhanced encounter count.
- Added an option to enable or disable common health and movement enhancements independently from dedicated behavior and identification.
- Made Enhanced selection less dependent on the order in which enemies appear.
- Changed Banger and Gnome to use group-level selection and consume one level-limit slot per group.
- Added a stronger green identification pulse when a player first approaches an Enhanced enemy.
- Improved Enhanced enemy stability while preserving common bonuses and visual identification.
- Reduced overlapping effects when supported single-enemy enhancement mods are installed.
- Reorganized REPOConfig settings into consistent global groups and alphabetical enemy sections.
